# Sophie Terms of Service

Last updated: October 15, 2026

## A private bot for one server

Sophie is a **private bot**. She is built, run and used **only in the r/bangladesh Discord server**, by that server's staff, for that server's members.

- Sophie is **not a public bot**. She cannot be invited to other servers, is not listed anywhere, and is not offered to anyone outside r/bangladesh.
- Her install link is not public. If she ever appears in another server, that is not authorized, and she will be removed from it.
- There is no paid tier, no sign-up, and no service offered to the public.

## Using Sophie

By talking to Sophie or chatting in channels where she is enabled in r/bangladesh, you agree to these terms and to the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

- Follow the r/bangladesh server rules and Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines when using her.
- Don't use Sophie to harass people, spread illegal content, or try to make her produce harmful material.
- Sophie is an AI. Her replies and fact-checks can be wrong. Don't treat them as professional, legal, medical or financial advice.
- Fact-check verdicts are her best reading of public sources at the time, not an official ruling by the server or its staff.

## Moderation

Sophie helps the r/bangladesh staff: she can warn members and report serious incidents (threats, doxxing, scams, content sexualizing minors) to a staff-only channel. All moderation decisions are made by the server's human moderators.

## Changes and availability

Sophie may be changed, paused or shut down at any time. These terms may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.

## Contact

Questions: open a support ticket in r/bangladesh or message a moderator.
