# Sophie Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Sophie is a **private Discord bot used only in the r/bangladesh Discord server**, run by its staff for its members. She is not a public bot and is not available in any other server (see the [Terms of Service](TERMS.md)). This policy explains what Sophie reads, what she keeps, and who else sees it.

## What Sophie reads

- Messages that mention her, reply to her, or are sent to her in DMs.
- Messages in channels where the server's moderators have turned her on, including ticket channels and channels with fact-checking enabled.
- Basic member info Discord provides: user ID, username, display name, roles, profile picture, banner, and presence (status and activity).
- Images attached to messages she reads, and profile pictures or banners when she is asked about them.

She does not read channels moderators have turned her off in, and she cannot see channels her role has no access to.

## What Sophie keeps

Nothing outside Discord. Sophie has no database or logs of her own. Everything she remembers lives inside the r/bangladesh Discord server:

- **Channel settings** set by moderators (mode, fact-checking, channel rules).
- **Short notes about members** that help her remember them, for example interests or running jokes. She never saves secrets or sensitive personal data.
- **Incident reports**, posted in a staff-only channel, which may include forwarded copies of messages as evidence.

Sophie is stateless once started. Everything she works with stays in memory (RAM), only for as long as it's needed to serve that conversation, and is never written to disk by us.

## Who else processes it

- **AI model provider.** To write replies, Sophie sends the recent conversation to our AI model provider under zero data retention: the provider does not store the data and does not use it for training.
- **Web search.** When Sophie looks something up or fact-checks a claim, the question or claim is sent to a web search service under zero data retention.

Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models.

## Your choices

- Ask Sophie or a moderator to delete notes she has saved about you, and they will be deleted.
- Moderators can turn Sophie off in any channel.
- Leaving the server stops Sophie from reading anything new from you.

## Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open a support ticket in r/bangladesh or message a moderator.
